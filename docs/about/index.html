About BAO

The Business Analytics Organization (BAO) is a student organization at the University of Illinois Chicago for anyone interested in how data informs business decisions. We bring students together to learn from one another, explore careers in analytics, and meet people working in the field.

What We Do

  • Industry panels to hear how professionals use analytics and how they got started.
  • Company office visits to see what work looks like across different teams and industries.
  • Hands-on workshops to practice working with data and try new tools.
  • Case competitions to tackle business problems in teams and present your recommendations.
  • Résumé and headshot days to get feedback on your résumé and take a professional photo.
  • Networking events and alumni connections to meet fellow students, UIC graduates, and industry professionals.
  • Opportunity matching to help you find opportunities that fit your interests and career goals.

We’re continuing to add new programs as our community grows.

Our Mission

Our mission is to support the intellectual and professional growth of UIC students interested in business analytics and consulting. We welcome students at every experience level and give them space to build skills, explore career paths, and connect with the analytics community.
